Privacy Policy – AIPrompter

Last updated: May 2026

Data We Collect

AIPrompter does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information.

Prompt Storage

Your prompt templates are saved locally in your browser using Chrome's storage.local API. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Permissions

AIPrompter requests the following permissions:

  • storage: To save and retrieve your prompt templates locally.
  • Content scripts on AI chat sites: To detect the "/" character and display the prompt dropdown on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

No data entered in AI chat inputs is read, recorded, or sent anywhere by this extension.

Third-Party Services

AIPrompter does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
